# Original braindump (founder notes)

*Captured 2026-07-14. Lightly formatted, content unchanged. This is the source of truth for intent — later docs elaborate but shouldn't contradict the spirit of this.*

- **Camera/presentation:** Manor Lords style map. You can zoom in to ground-level view or zoom out to sky-high.
- **World creation:** Either start with a blank map and do fantasy map building, or pick presets.
- **Core fantasy:** You are essentially playing god from Year 0. You aren't playing against anyone, you aren't fighting anyone, you aren't trying to beat anyone. This is purely god-perspective worldbuilding.
- **Emergent reactions to interventions:**
  - Spawn gold into a mountain → gold miners set up camp, build mining villages, get rich, etc.
  - Spawn horrible weather into a zone → people don't live there.
  - Spawn a dragon into 2026 New York City → watch the world burn.
- **Settings:** Can go for a real-life play or a fantasy play.
- **Faith as currency (explicitly flagged as "not sure, brainstorm this"):**
  - People praying to you could be a currency.
  - Must NOT be a simple, one-toned, streamlined thing.
  - People can start to believe in other gods and spawn rival religions you compete with.
  - The world behaves based on your actions: they might fear you if you're evil, pray to you if you're merciful.
  - The world's *look* should respond too — darker if you're evil, etc.
  - The world needs to be dynamic and feel alive, "like you are actually god himself."
- **Depth for those who want it:** The game tracks lore, cultures, religions, fame, stand-out people throughout the years. A committed player's universe could be 4,000 years old — warlords, revolutionaries, etc.
- **Multiplayer:** Imagine spawning on opposite sides of a world, cultures meet, other interesting multiplayer loops.
- **Art:** On the more realistic side of things. Question: can Claude Code / Blender MCP produce the models and artwork so we don't need to source them elsewhere?
- **Engine question:** UE5 / Unity / other?
- **Audience note:** A decent part of the target audience is nerdy, D&D-liking, map-building, creative-writing nerds — but also great co-op multiplayer and streamability. A chill game.
